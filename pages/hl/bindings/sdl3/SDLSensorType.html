@:enum

The type of a sensor.

Sensors are classified by what they measure. Some devices (e.g. Nintendo Joy-Cons) expose pairs of the same type, one on the left half and one on the right half of the controller; those are represented by the _L and _R variants.

Corresponds to SDL_SensorType in SDL3.
